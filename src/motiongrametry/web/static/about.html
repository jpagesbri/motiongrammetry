About motiongrametry

motiongrametry turns a phone video walking around something into a clean, textured 3D model, a 3D-print file and more. It is built on the work of many open-source projects and research groups, listed below with their licenses. Each is the work of its authors and is used under its own license; the full texts are one click away here, and all of them are in one file for redistribution:

Download all notices (THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt) or on the command line: mg notices -o THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.txt

The VGGT code is under the VGGT License: anyone you pass it on to must get a copy of that license, and publications using it should acknowledge it. The rest of the app does not need VGGT.

If you distribute that image, or let others use it over a network, offer them its source: the v2.3.0 tag (and VCGLib, GPL-3.0, compiled into it). Changing OpenMVS itself would mean publishing those changes under the AGPL too.
